projects

Optical-trapping & DDM analysis pipeline

A structured xarray Dataset holding optical trapping and DDM data across trap count, laser power, and trial dimensions. Includes a "closest pair" outlier-detection method built for small sample sizes, interactive domain-by-domain review tooling for flagging and NaN-ing bad points, and correct error propagation through the nonlinear 1/τ transform.

Homelab & agentic AI infrastructure

Self-hosted stack on Proxmox VE and TrueNAS SCALE: Jellyfin, Nextcloud, Vaultwarden, a VPN'd qBittorrent + *arr stack, and Tailscale for remote access. Currently extending it with Ollama, Open WebUI, and n8n to explore agentic AI running on local hardware, with a repurposed gaming laptop earmarked as an inference node and a home sensor network (starting with garden irrigation) planned next.
